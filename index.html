Academic & Engineering Profile

Bridging Rigorous Scientific Research with Real-World Cyber-Physical Systems

I am an academic lecturer at Universitas Pendidikan Nasional (Undiknas) and a PhD researcher at Wageningen University & Research (WUR). Through Narayana Instrument Laboratory (Narin-Laboratory), I engineer robust cyber-physical hardware and software instruments for precision agriculture.

My flagship initiative is UDAWA (Universal Digital Agriculture Workflow Assistant)—a modular ecosystem spanning greenhouse microclimate surveillance, automated hydroponic nutrient dosing, AC electrical metering, on-chip LittleFS web interfaces, and centralized cloud telemetry.

Complementing my IoT research, I author AI platforms like SLR-Magic to automate Systematic Literature Reviews with local-first LLM precision, as well as creative productivity tools such as Outliner and lirius.

Integrated Cyber-Physical Framework

The UDAWA Smart Agriculture Architecture

UDAWA (Universal Digital Agriculture Workflow Assistant) unites distributed edge microcontrollers, on-chip web servers, and cloud analytics into a resilient, multi-tier precision farming network.

01

Edge Sensor & Actuator Layer

Hardware microcontrollers running FreeRTOS directly inside greenhouse bays:

UDAWA-Damodar: EC, TDS, water temp & nutrient pumps.
UDAWA-Murari: Ambient temp, RH & VPD misting regulation.
UDAWA-Gadadar: AC relay actuators & real-time power monitoring.
02

Edge Computing & Local Access

Guaranteed field resilience during network or internet outages:

UDAWA-Embedded-WebIface:

Lightweight web server hosted inside ESP32 LittleFS flash. Enables offline sensor calibration, Wi-Fi captive portal setup, and direct relay overrides without external internet connectivity.

03

Cloud Telemetry & Analytics

Centralized oversight, cross-farm analytics, and historical logging:

UDAWA-Cloud-WebIface:

Full-stack web application with MQTT/WebSockets, graphical dashboards, historical trend reporting, multi-node telemetry synchronization, and proactive anomaly alert broadcasts.
